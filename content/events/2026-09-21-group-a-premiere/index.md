Step onto the purple carpet, then head into a theater packed with filmmakers, friends, and fans. Ticket check-in and purple carpet begin **one hour before the screening**. Each film screens once, with live Audience Choice voting at the end of the night.

## Screening Group A

- **Ad Mortem** by Team Roller Pictures — Romantic Comedy and/or Romance
- **The Case of the Missing Jewels** by And Company! Productions — Film Noir and/or Single Room Movie
- **Charlie** by The 2880 Minute Movie Makers — Drama and/or Workplace Film
- **Detour** by Crab Factory — Dark Comedy and/or Vacation / Holiday Film
- **Mercy** by iBuddy Productions — Fish Out of Water and/or Moral Dilemma
- **Miss Engagement** by House of Kirby — Road Movie and/or Buddy Film
- **Nice to Meat You** by Carlling Productions — Family Film and/or Horror
- **Other Skills** by Almost Good Films X Jabronie Pictures — Film de Femme and/or Silent Film
- **Persons** by Made by MHADE — Sci Fi and/or Misunderstanding
- **Recount** by Cacti — Film de Femme and/or Sports Film / Game Film
- **Revenge of the Tourist** by Steele Canyon Studios — Mockumentary and/or Revenge
- **The Station at the End of the World** by IntelleXual Entertainment — Thriller / Suspense and/or Utopian or Dystopian
- **Threads of Amoria** by Neuro Spice Brigade — Fantasy and/or Food Film
- **Veloci-boyfriend** by Dog Jaw Productions — Fish Out of Water and/or Single Room Movie
