<h1>Become a Sponsor</h1>

<p>The San Diego 48 Hour Film Project brings together creative teams of all
skill levels to make short films in just one weekend. Throughout the year we
host mixers, open auditions, a kickoff event, premiere screenings, and an
awards gala — all opportunities to connect your brand with a passionate,
community-driven audience.</p>

<p>Sponsorship helps us keep registration affordable, rent great venues, and
celebrate local filmmaking on the big screen. In return, sponsors receive
visibility on our website, at events, and across our social channels.</p>

<p>See our current sponsors on the <a href="/partners/">Partners page</a>.</p>

<h2>Sponsorship Packages</h2>

<p>Every package can be tailored to your goals. <strong>Contact us</strong> to
discuss pricing, event opportunities, and custom benefits.</p>

<h2>In-Kind Sponsorship</h2>

<p>We also welcome in-kind contributions such as venue space, food and beverage,

recognition appropriate to the value and visibility of their contribution.</p>
